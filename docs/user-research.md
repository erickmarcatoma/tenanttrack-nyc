# User research: tenant interviews

Goal: 5 interviews with NYC tenants who have dealt with repair problems.

## Recruiting

- Tenant associations
- Legal-aid clinics
- Community boards and local organizations

## Interview guide (30 min)

1. Tell me about the last repair problem in your apartment or building.
2. What did you do first? Who did you contact?
3. Have you ever called 311 or contacted HPD? What happened?
4. How did you know whether the landlord was supposed to fix it, and by when?
5. What was the hardest part?
6. Which language do you prefer for this kind of information?
7. *(Show prototype)* Look up your building. What do you see? What would you do next?
8. Would you send a letter drafted by the app? What would make you trust it?
